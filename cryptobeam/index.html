It started in the menu bar.

CryptoBeam began as a Mac menu-bar portfolio tracker: one click to see what you hold, then back to whatever you were doing. It still works that way, and a token or a DeFi position opens in its own window for the details.

Its iPhone and iPad apps bring that same private portfolio view to the rest of your Apple devices. Give it a wide window, on iPad or iPhone Duo, and the portfolio spreads into two or three columns, with details beside the list.

It is still built by one independent developer, after bedtime, with grit, willpower, and a ridiculous amount of coffee. Your purchase helps keep the updates coming. Consider it a digital buy-me-a-coffee.

Designed differently, on purpose

Your portfolio is your business.

There is no CryptoBeam backend. The app talks only to the providers you configure, and iCloud sync runs through your personal Apple account. CryptoBeam never asks for seed phrases, wallet private keys, or transaction-signing access.

Want a lock on top? Turn on Require Face ID in Settings on iPhone or iPad, and CryptoBeam asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode when you open or come back to the app.
