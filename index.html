How we operate

Built with a few hard rules

🔒 No servers, no snooping

Our apps run on your device. We don't operate servers that collect your data, because we'd rather not have it.

📵 No tracking, no ads

No analytics pipelines, no ad networks, no behavioral profiles. What you do in the app stays in the app.

🧢 Independent, on purpose

Small studio, no outside investors telling us to monetize your attention. We build what we'd want to use.

☕ Powered by caffeine

And the occasional dad joke. We take the software seriously. We don't take ourselves too seriously.
